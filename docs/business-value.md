# Business value and pilot measurement

SignaFlow AI is an independent reference implementation for improving catalogue discovery, sales workflows, and digital-signage measurement. It does not assume or claim revenue improvement before a controlled pilot.

## Value hypotheses

| Existing strength | Opportunity | AI enhancement | Potential business benefit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Broad specialist catalogue | Customer may not know technical names | intent-led product discovery | shorter search and response time |
| Experienced sales team | Qualification quality can vary by enquiry | progressive structured requirements | clearer priorities and fewer repetitive questions |
| Complete signage capability | Accessories and services can be missed | compatibility-aware solution builder | more relevant cross-sell and fewer incomplete solutions |
| Established quotation process | Drafting repeats known calculations | database-backed quote drafting | faster turnaround with approval retained |
| Multiple customer touchpoints | Context can fragment | shared requirement-to-lead journey | smoother customer and salesperson handoff |
| Digital signage installations | Audience response is not always visible | privacy-preserving aggregate analytics | measurable campaign experiments |

## Pilot KPIs

1. **Product search time** — median time from requirement received to reviewed shortlist.
2. **Lead response time** — median time to first meaningful response, segmented by lead temperature.
3. **Quote turnaround** — time from qualified requirement to reviewed draft.
4. **Lead-to-quote conversion** — qualified leads that receive a quote.
5. **Products per quotation** — compatible primary/support/optional items per reviewed quote.
6. **Relevant cross-sell acceptance** — accepted accessories/services divided by proposed compatible additions.
7. **Follow-up consistency** — leads contacted within the agreed SLA.
8. **Campaign engagement** — approved campaign tests measured through anonymous ROI metrics.

## Suggested pilot

- Select two catalogue categories and one sales team.
- Import reviewed product facts and compatibility rules.
- Record a two- to four-week baseline before changing workflow.
- Run AI-assisted and existing processes side by side.
- Require salesperson approval for every quote and outbound draft.
- Review recommendation relevance, missing-information behavior, and failure cases weekly.
- Make a rollout decision from measured time, quality, conversion, and governance outcomes.

## Guardrails

The pilot should never interpret prototype metrics as business results. Smart Signage requires privacy/legal review, retention rules, site communication, and aggregate-only access. Pricing, discounts, delivery, warranty, certification, and installation commitments remain with authorized business users.
