# SignaFlow AI — 5–7 minute portfolio demo

## 0:00–0:40 — Frame the opportunity

Open the home page.

“SignaFlow AI demonstrates one intelligent journey across product discovery, quotation, sales follow-up, campaign creation, and measurement for a fictional signage business. The goal is not a chatbot—it is a requirement-to-revenue operating layer.”

Point out the connected journey: Understand → Recommend → Configure → Qualify → Quote → Engage → Measure.

## 0:40–2:00 — Customer Product Advisor

Open **AI Product Advisor** and use the preloaded request:

> I am opening a restaurant in Dubai. I need three indoor digital menu screens and one outdoor advertising screen.

Run the advisor.

Show the extracted structure: Restaurant, Dubai, both environments, 3 indoor, 1 outdoor, menus + advertising. Explain that the advisor asks only one question when a decision-changing fact is absent.

Show the three catalogue-grounded product recommendations, confidence, matched signals, missing-specification safeguards, and Demo Product labels.

## 2:00–2:45 — Complete solution and qualified lead

Click **Build complete solution**.

Show the indoor and outdoor displays, media players, CMS, mounting provisions, optional high-brightness upgrade, and compatibility/site-survey notes.

Click **Request quotation**. The demo contact, AED 35,000 budget, and two-week timeline are prefilled; submit them.

Show **Hot — 100/100**. Explain that the number is deterministic Python logic, not an LLM opinion.

## 2:45–3:50 — CRM, Copilot, and quotation

Open the new lead in CRM. Show the original requirement, budget, contact details, and score explanation.

Click **Summarize lead**, then **Draft WhatsApp**. Emphasize that messages are drafts and are never sent automatically.

Click **Prepare quotation**. Open **Quotations**, show database pricing, 5% VAT, missing-price flag behavior, and download the PDF. Point out the DRAFT / SALES REVIEW REQUIRED marking.

## 3:50–4:50 — Campaign Studio

Open **Campaign Studio** with Restaurant, Weekend Burger Offer, Buy 1 Get 1 Free, Families, English + Arabic.

Generate the campaign. Switch through the three code-native creative variants and change the screen format between portrait, landscape, LED banner, and menu board. Show the bilingual-ready/RTL behavior, display duration, and schedule recommendation.

Reiterate: generation is allowed; publishing is approval-gated.

## 4:50–5:35 — Analytics and agentic optimization

Open **Analytics**. Show simulated impressions, anonymous estimated viewers, engagement, hourly audience trend, and campaign comparison.

Show the optimization cards: the agent proposes a schedule experiment with supporting metrics and confidence, but cannot change a production schedule.

## 5:35–6:20 — Smart Signage

Open **Smart Signage**. Choose any local demo video name or use the default and click **Analyze anonymous traffic**.

Show people passed, people inside the screen viewing ROI, estimated engagement, average dwell, and the timeline chart.

State explicitly: “No facial recognition, no identity, no biometrics, and no sensitive-attribute inference.”

## 6:20–7:00 — Close on value

Open **Business Impact**.

“The next step is a measured pilot. We establish baselines for product-search time, lead response, quote turnaround, conversion, and relevant cross-sell. These are potential KPIs—not claimed results. The prototype proves the connected workflow can continue even without Groq, internet, scraping, or a downloaded vision model.”
